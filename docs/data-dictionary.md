# Pilot Data Dictionary

## Node status

- `operating`: commercial production currently reported.
- `development`: resource/project with no reported construction start.
- `construction`: physical construction reported.
- `care_and_maintenance`: preserved but not producing.
- `suspended`: interruption with potential restart.
- `closed`: permanently closed or rehabilitated.
- `unknown`: evidence is insufficient or stale.

## Provenance

- `reported`: explicitly stated by a traceable source.
- `modelled`: reproducible calculation from cited reported inputs.
- `unverified`: insufficient evidence for either category.

## Quantity rule

Never combine ore tonnes, concentrate tonnes, contained Sb, recovered Sb, metal and oxide. Every quantity must retain product form, original unit, period and source assertion.
